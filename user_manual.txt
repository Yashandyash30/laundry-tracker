ARIES Hostel Laundry Tracker

Welcome to our new digital system for managing the shared washing machines. No more climbing stairs just to find them busy!
1. Check & Plan
   Open the Link: https://aries-laundry.streamlit.app

   Status at a Glance:

        Green: Machine is free.

        Red: Machine is busy. The app tells you exactly who is using it and when it will be free (e.g., “Expected: 10:30 PM”).

2. Start Your Wash

    If Free: Load your clothes, select the machine in the app, and click "Start Machine".

    Set Your PIN: Create a simple 4-digit PIN (e.g., 1234).

        Note: You need this PIN to stop the timer or extend time if the power goes out!

3. The Queue

If a machine is busy or has an existing waitlist, click "Join Queue".

    Join Details: Enter your Name, Designation (PhD/PDF/Project Student/Visitor), optional note, and your PIN.

    Urgency & Moving Higher in Queue:
        - If you have an emergency (e.g., travel/presentation, no clean clothes left), tick the "🔥 Urgent" box and state your reason.
        - Marking urgent does not automatically jump the line. Instead, everyone above you will see your request and have the option to move you higher in the queue!
        - Moving Someone Higher: Click your name in the queue ➝ Enter YOUR PIN ➝ Click "▼ Swap Down". This boosts the person below you up one position into your higher slot.
        - Note: Helping a friend in need is voluntary, and multiple residents above can step down to move an urgent user to the front.

    Leave Queue: If you washed by hand or plans changed, click your name in the queue ➝ Enter YOUR PIN ➝ Click "❌ Leave" to free up the line.

    Claiming Your Turn:
        - When the machine finishes, it is held exclusively for the 1st person in queue for 15 minutes.
        - Click "Start (Your Name)" to claim it. Nobody else can start during your window.

    Timeouts & Skip:
        - If the 15-minute window expires without claiming, single users are auto-removed.
        - If multiple people are queued, a "🚀 Skip to [Next Person]" button appears so the next resident can take over without waiting.

4. Stay Updated

    Browser: Click "Enable Alerts" in the sidebar and keep the tab open for a popup when your machine is ready.

    Telegram: Join the ARIES Laundry Updates group (https://t.me/+69YKX5iyRyM1NzJl) to get instant notifications on your phone even when the app is closed.

5. Troubleshooting

    Power Cut? Go to "Manage" ➝ Enter PIN ➝ "Add Time" (e.g., +15 mins).

    Forgot PIN? Contact Yash (Admin) for a reset.


Important : Time’s Up? Please collect clothes within 15 mins of finish time, or the next person has the right to skip your turn.
